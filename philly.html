Meet Philly

Fun facts about Philly

  • Philly was born March 17, 2026.
  • He loves to play fetch and go on long walks.
  • He is very loyal and protective.
  • Philly's favorite thing to chew on is sticks and stuffed toys.

Philly is a pit mix who is half Dogo Argentino. My family and I have owned him for 5 months. He is very energetic, loves to play fetch, and is always by my side. Philly has a strong bond with my dad, who is his favorite person, and is known for his protective nature. Even though Philly looks intimidating, he is very goofy and cuddly. So far Philly is learning new tricks and becoming a well-behaved dog in the family.
