Meet Kilo

Fun facts about Kilo

  • Kilo was born June 2, 2018.
  • He loves to chew bones and bark at people.
  • He is very small but full of energy.
  • Kilo was rejected by his mom at 2 weeks old and had to be bottle-fed.

Kilo is a small black-and-white Chihuahua who I have owned for 8 years. He is very energetic, protective, and loves to model the thousands of bandanas I put on him. Kilo was given to me when one of my best friends' dogs had puppies and the mother rejected him from the litter. He has been through thick and thin with my family, and I wouldn't trade him for the world.
